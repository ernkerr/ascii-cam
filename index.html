ascii-cam

Turn your webcam or any photo into live ASCII art, right in your browser. Everything runs locally on your device — nothing is uploaded. Pick from ten character sets (or type your own), choose colors, adjust contrast and resolution, then export your creation as a PNG image, MP4 video, or animated GIF. Free, no signups, no ads.

How it works

  1. Allow camera access, or upload a photo instead.
  2. Pick a character set, color, and contrast — the ASCII art updates live.
  3. Export a PNG snapshot, record an MP4 video, or capture a GIF.
